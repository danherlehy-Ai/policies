Program name

Daniel Herlehy Call-Back Texts.

Program description

When you call (716) 325-4843 and we are unable to answer, our system may send you a one-time courtesy text message to let you know your call was received and will be returned. These are conversational follow-up messages only — not marketing.

How you enroll

You receive a message only because you placed a phone call to our number first. There is no website form or keyword sign-up; texts are sent only to the number that called us.

Message frequency & cost

Message frequency varies (at most one message per missed call). Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out & help

Text STOP to cancel at any time and you will no longer receive messages. Text HELP for help, or call (716) 325-4843 for support.

Privacy

Your information is handled per our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

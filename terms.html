Program name

Daniel Herlehy Call Follow-Up Texts.

Program description

When you call (716) 325-4843, our automated phone assistant answers your call. During the call, the assistant asks whether you would like to receive a one-time text message confirming that your call was received and that Daniel Herlehy will follow up. A text message is sent only if you clearly say yes during the call. These are one-time, conversational customer-care messages only — no marketing, no promotions, no recurring messages.

How you enroll (opt-in)

Consent is collected verbally during your inbound phone call. Every caller who is offered a text message hears the following before any message is sent:

"Would you like me to send you a one-time text message confirming that your call was received and that Dan will follow up? Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help."

If you say no, hang up, or do not answer the question, no text message is sent. Messages are sent only to the phone number that placed the call. We never use purchased lists, web forms, or third-party numbers, and consent is not required to purchase any goods or services.

Message frequency & cost

At most one (1) message per consented inbound call. This is not a recurring messaging program. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out & help

Text STOP to cancel at any time and you will receive no further messages. Text HELP for help, or call (716) 325-4843 for support.

Your information is handled per our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

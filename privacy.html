Privacy Policy

Daniel Herlehy · Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daniel Herlehy ("we," "us") handles information in connection with the phone line (716) 325-4843 and its automated call-back text messages.

Information we collect

When you call our phone line, we collect your phone number (caller ID) and any message or details you provide during the call. We do not collect any other personal information.

How we use it

We use your phone number solely to return your call and to send you a one-time courtesy text message letting you know your call was received and will be returned. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be processed only by the service providers that operate this phone/messaging service (for example, our telephony provider, Twilio) strictly to deliver the service to you.

Text messaging & opt-out

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies (at most one message per missed call). You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or call (716) 325-4843.

Data retention

Call and message records are retained only as long as needed to follow up with you and are not used for any other purpose.

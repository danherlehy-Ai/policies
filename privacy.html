Privacy Policy

Daniel Herlehy · Last updated: 2026-06-10

This Privacy Policy explains how Daniel Herlehy ("we," "us") handles information in connection with the phone line (716) 325-4843 and its one-time call follow-up text messages.

Information we collect

When you call our phone line, we collect your phone number (caller ID), any message or details you provide during the call, and your verbal response when our phone assistant asks whether you would like a one-time text follow-up. We do not collect any other personal information.

How we use it

We use your phone number solely to return your call and — only if you said yes during the call — to send you a single text message confirming your call was received and will be returned. We do not send marketing or promotional messages, and we do not send any text message without your verbal consent during the call.

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be processed only by the service providers that operate this phone/messaging service (for example, our telephony provider, Twilio) strictly to deliver the service to you.

Text messaging & opt-out

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency: at most one (1) message per consented inbound call; this is not a recurring messaging program. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or call (716) 325-4843. Full program terms: SMS Terms & Conditions.

Consent records

We log the call timestamp, caller phone number, and verbal consent result for each consented message, retained only as proof of opt-in.

Data retention

Call and message records are retained only as long as needed to follow up with you and to document consent, and are not used for any other purpose.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call (716) 325-4843.

What this program is

Daniel Herlehy operates a single business phone line, (716) 325-4843, published to customers, contacts, and the public. When someone places a phone call to this number and the call is not answered live, our system may send that caller one courtesy text message back to the same mobile number they called from, letting them know their call was received and will be returned.

How consent is obtained

Consent is consumer-initiated. The recipient provides their mobile number and initiates contact by voluntarily calling our published business number first. The courtesy text is a direct, one-time reply to that inbound call. We do not text anyone who has not called us. We do not use any purchased list, web form, bulk upload, or third-party source — the only numbers we message are the numbers that called our line.

Example of the experience:
A person calls (716) 325-4843. The call is not answered live. A few minutes later they receive a single text such as: “Hi, sorry I missed your call — I’ll get back to you as soon as I can. — Dan (Daniel Herlehy). Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”

Message frequency & rates

Message frequency is limited to at most one message per missed call. These are conversational, one-time courtesy replies — not marketing or promotional messages. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out & help

Reply STOP at any time to opt out and receive no further messages. Reply HELP, or call (716) 325-4843, for help.

Privacy

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Full details are in our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms & Conditions.
